# AI Lead Intelligence Engine Methodology & Formulation
## AI Power Sales Intelligence System (Version 2.0)

### 1. Abstract & Academic Disclaimer
In academic and commercial presentations, artificial intelligence implementations are frequently presented as opaque "black-box" systems. In enterprise customer relationship management, untraceable black-box scores diminish sales representative trust and violate explainability requirements.

The **AI Sales Intelligence Engine v2.0** uses a **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)** scoring architecture paired with an **Anti-Repeat Rule Induction Engine**. Every score output is deterministic, fully explainable, and decomposed into individual criteria vectors.

> **Honesty & Transparency Statement:**  
> The conversion probabilities and scores generated by this version are mathematical heuristics derived from established B2B sales qualification criteria (BANT and MEDDIC frameworks). They do NOT represent a statistically trained neural network or regression model fit on proprietary historical datasets. No unverified statistical claims are made.

---

### 2. Mathematical Scoring Formulation
The total Lead Intelligence Score $S \in [0, 100]$ is defined as the weighted linear combination of six normalized feature evaluation functions:

$$S = \min\left(100, \max\left(0, \sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i \cdot \phi_i(L)\right)\right)$$

Where the weight vector satisfies $\sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i^{\max} = 100$:

| Index $i$ | Criteria Dimension | Max Weight $w_i^{\max}$ | Input Vector Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Seniority Authority** | 15 pts | `lead.jobTitle` |
| 2 | **Industry Segment Fit** | 15 pts | `lead.industry` |
| 3 | **Deal Value Potential** | 20 pts | `lead.dealValue` |
| 4 | **Engagement & Recency** | 20 pts | `lead.lastContactedAt`, `activities.length` |
| 5 | **Profile Completeness** | 15 pts | `name`, `email`, `phone`, `company`, `notes` |
| 6 | **Channel Quality** | 15 pts | `lead.source` |

---

### 3. Feature Evaluation Functions $\phi_i(L)$

#### A. Seniority Authority $\phi_1(L) \in [3, 15]$
Identifies organizational decision-making power:
- **C-Suite / Founders** (`CEO`, `CTO`, `CFO`, `CRO`, `President`, `Managing Partner`): **15 pts** (Positive Impact)
- **Senior Leadership** (`VP`, `Vice President`, `Head`, `Director`): **12 pts** (Positive Impact)
- **Mid-Level Management** (`Manager`, `Lead`, `Principal`, `Architect`): **8 pts** (Neutral Impact)
- **Individual Contributor** (`Specialist`, `Analyst`, `Engineer`, `Associate`): **5 pts** (Neutral Impact)
- **Generic / Unverified**: **3 pts** (Negative Impact)

#### B. Industry Fit $\phi_2(L) \in [4, 15]$
Evaluates alignment with high-conversion sectors:
- **Tier 1** (`Technology`, `Finance`, `Healthcare`): **15 pts**
- **Tier 2** (`Manufacturing`, `Logistics`, `Professional Services`): **11 pts**
- **Tier 3** (`Retail`, `Education`, `Real Estate`): **7 pts**
- **Unclassified**: **4 pts**

#### C. Deal Value Potential $\phi_3(L) \in [3, 20]$
Evaluates enterprise pipeline impact:
- $V \ge \$50,000$: **20 pts**
- $\$20,000 \le V < \$50,000$: **16 pts**
- $\$7,500 \le V < \$20,000$: **12 pts**
- $\$2,000 \le V < \$7,500$: **7 pts**
- $V < \$2,000$: **3 pts**

#### D. Engagement & Recency $\phi_4(L) \in [2, 20]$
Measures interaction momentum and penalizes stale accounts:
- $\ge 4$ activities and contacted $\le 3$ days ago: **20 pts**
- $\ge 2$ activities and contacted $\le 7$ days ago: **16 pts**
- Fresh inquiry created $\le 3$ days ago: **12 pts**
- Recent touchpoint $\le 14$ days ago: **13 pts**
- Cooling account (inactive 15–30 days): **6 pts**
- Stale account (inactive $> 30$ days): **2 pts**

#### E. Profile Completeness $\phi_5(L) \in [0, 15]$
Accumulates 3 points for each verified attribute: Contact Name, Verified Email, Company Name, Direct Telephone, Context Notes.

#### F. Channel Quality $\phi_6(L) \in [4, 15]$
Evaluates acquisition channel trust and historical close probability:
- `Referral`, `Partner`: **15 pts**
- `Inbound Form`, `Website`, `Organic Search`: **12 pts**
- `Event`, `LinkedIn`: **9 pts**
- `Cold Outreach`: **6 pts**
- `Other`: **4 pts**

### 4. Classification & Conversion Likelihood Heuristic

#### Tier Classification
- **Hot Opportunity**: $S \ge 75$ (Immediate high-touch sales prioritization)
- **Warm Opportunity**: $45 \le S < 75$ (Nurturing track, active deal progression)
- **Cold Opportunity**: $S < 45$ (Early inbound or disengaged account)

#### Conversion Likelihood Heuristic Formula
The estimated conversion likelihood $P_c \in [1\%, 99\%]$ is calculated by scaling the base AI score with stage-specific advancement multipliers:

$$P_c = \text{clamp}\left(\text{round}\left((0.6 \cdot S + 20) \times M_{\text{stage}}\right), 1, 99\right)$$

Stage multipliers $M_{\text{stage}}$:
- `New`: $0.70$
- `Contacted`: $0.85$
- `Qualified`: $1.00$
- `Proposal`: $1.20$
- `Negotiation`: $1.40$
- `Won`: Force $100\%$
- `Lost`: Force $5\%$

### 5. Next-Best-Action Recommendation Engine
To prevent redundant recommendations, the engine inspects the lead's last three recorded activities before emitting a recommendation:
1. **Negotiation Stage**: Finalize commercial SLA and contract terms (Urgent priority).
2. **Proposal Stage**: If a meeting occurred within 5 days, recommend delivering customized pricing documents; otherwise schedule a proposal review workshop.
3. **Hot Lead Tier**: If no recent phone interaction exists, recommend an executive priority call; if a call has occurred, recommend a solution architecture demo.
4. **Stale Opportunity ($> 14$ days inactivity)**: Deploy an industry-specific case study re-engagement touchpoint.
5. **New Lead**: Recommend phone verification if a direct number is present; otherwise initiate personalized discovery email.

---

### 6. Future External AI Integration Architecture
The intelligence layer is encapsulated inside `server/services/aiEngine.js`. To integrate modern LLM APIs (such as Gemini 1.5 Pro):
1. Create an asynchronous client wrapper calling `GoogleGenerativeAI`.
2. Construct a prompt injecting the lead's JSON profile and recent activity stream.
3. Require structured JSON output returning `{ score, rationale, factors, nextBestAction }`.
4. Fall back to the deterministic MCDA algorithm if the external API is unreachable or rate-limited.
5. Store external API keys strictly in `server/.env`.
